CLARITY FIRST

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as an event in FlowTrace? expand_more

An event corresponds to a single span record, transaction checkpoint, or diagnostic anomaly trace sent through our client SDKs (e.g., tracer.span() or OpenTelemetry OTLP packets). Heartbeats and topology keep-alive pings are completely free and unmetered.

What happens when my system hits the monthly quota limit? expand_more

We never silently penalize or charge your card for burst spikes. When your workspace hits 100%, our ingest service activates a 24-hour grace buffer that safely holds incoming events. You will receive an automated Slack or email alert with the option to upgrade instantly or let the buffer safely drain.

Can we self-host FlowTrace on our own VPC or Kubernetes cluster? expand_more

Yes. The Business tier includes an enterprise Helm chart and Docker Compose suite verified on AWS EKS, Google Cloud GKE, and Azure AKS. In self-hosted setups, raw trace payloads never leave your cloud perimeter, while configuration state syncs securely.

How does the annual discount work? expand_more

Choosing annual billing provides a direct 2-month bonus (~17% discount). For example, the Pro plan is reduced from €19/mo to €15/mo (invoiced at €180/year), and the Team tier drops from €79/mo to €65/mo.

Does FlowTrace add latency to our application runtime? expand_more

No. Our instrumentation SDKs use non-blocking background daemons and lightweight circular ring buffers over UDP or asynchronous HTTP/2 multiplexing. The median overhead benchmarked in production is <0.18ms per trace span.

Can I change or cancel my plan at any time? expand_more

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time directly in your organization settings. Plan adjustments are prorated down to the second. If you downgrade, your telemetry history remains available until the end of your billing cycle.
