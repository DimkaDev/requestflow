Technical FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for software architects and DevOps leads.

What counts as an event in FlowTrace? expand_more
Each individual span, checkpoint, or structured log packet dispatched via our standard client libraries (e.g., tracer.span() or UDP daemon frame) counts as exactly 1 event. Batch calls containing 50 spans count as 50 events. Heartbeats and health checks emitted by the local daemon are free and never deducted from your quota.
What happens when my system hits the monthly quota limit? expand_more
Unlike traditional observability vendors that bill punitive 3x overage rates, FlowTrace protects your credit card with hard quota protection. When 100% is reached, ingestion pauses gracefully and writes incoming packets to an encrypted 24-hour circular buffer. You can upgrade with a single click to flush the buffer into your live trace explorer without losing telemetry data.
Can we self-host FlowTrace on our own VPC or Kubernetes cluster? expand_more
Yes. On our Business & Enterprise plans, we supply production-ready Helm charts and Terraform templates compatible with AWS EKS, GCP GKE, and on-premises bare-metal setups. Your telemetry stays 100% within your network boundary for stringent banking and healthcare compliance.
How does the annual discount work? expand_more
When you select annual billing, you receive 12 full months of observability for the price of 10. That equates to an immediate ~17% discount applied automatically at checkout. Invoicing and custom tax identifiers (VAT / GST) are standard.
Does FlowTrace add latency to our application runtime? expand_more
Negligible impact. FlowTrace SDKs transmit traces over local non-blocking Unix sockets or asynchronous ring-buffers via UDP to a lightweight local sidecar. Total overhead benchmarks at under 0.18ms per trace boundary with zero garbage collector pauses.
Can I change or cancel my plan at any time? expand_more
Yes. Downgrades and upgrades take effect immediately with automated second-by-second prorated credits applied back to your FlowTrace balance. You can cancel your subscription anytime with a simple self-serve toggle in your Organization Settings.
