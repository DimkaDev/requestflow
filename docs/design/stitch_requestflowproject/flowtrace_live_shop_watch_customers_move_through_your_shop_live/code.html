Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about setting up FlowTrace on your storefront.

Will FlowTrace slow down my online store?

expand_more
No! FlowTrace runs completely asynchronously via ultra-lightweight edge telemetry (under 3KB total). It connects straight to background websocket buffers and never blocks the checkout thread or delays Core Web Vitals.

Do I need a developer to install it?

expand_more
If you use Shopify or WooCommerce, install with one click directly from their official app stores. For custom headless setups (Next.js, Medusa, Astro), you simply paste a tiny 2-line snippet or embed our open SDK.

Is customer privacy protected?

expand_more
Yes, strictly. We never record personal names, credit cards, full street addresses, or keystroke inputs. Shoppers are represented purely by friendly anonymous animal avatars, preserving complete GDPR, CCPA, and SOC2 compliance.

Can I display this on a smart TV in our office or warehouse?

expand_more
Yes! Just open the TV Showroom link in any smart TV web browser (LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, Android TV, or via Apple TV/Chromecast) and hit full screen. The dashboard automatically hides the mouse cursor and features anti-burn-in pixel shifting.

What happens if we have a viral surge in sales?

expand_more
We never drop shopping events or lock your storefront during sudden traffic spikes. FlowTrace uses horizontally auto-scaling serverless edge clusters that seamlessly handle tens of thousands of concurrent checkouts without breaking a sweat.
